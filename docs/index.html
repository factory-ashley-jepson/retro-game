Build your own morning brief

In 30 minutes, you'll build an assistant that reads your email and calendar every weekday morning and tells you what needs you: today's meetings with prep notes, emails waiting on you, and promises you made.

Open this page on your laptop: factory-ashley-jepson.github.io/morning-brief

Get set up 5 minutes

Do these before we start if you can. Raise your hand if anything gets stuck.

  1. Download the Factory app and sign in

    Open it, then sign in or create a free account.

    Mac (Apple chip) Mac (Intel) Windows Windows (ARM)

  2. Add tonight's credits

    Your code is on the screen up front.

  3. Connect your email and calendar

    Work account blocked? Your company may require admin approval. Use a personal account, or start with sample data and connect later.

  4. Install the workshop kit

Build it like a real team 25 minutes

Start a new session, then go through four steps. Type each command in the session.

StepTypeWhat happens
1. Plan/brief-setupDroid checks your connections, asks four quick questions, and writes your brief profile: what you care about, who matters most, and when it arrives.
2. Build/morning-briefDroid writes your first brief from your real inbox and calendar.
3. Test/brief-checkDroid checks every item against its source, runs a 10-point checklist, and fixes what failed.
4. Ship/brief-scheduleDroid turns it into an automation that emails you the brief every weekday morning.
No accounts connected yet? Say "use sample data" in step 1 to build and test with a fictional inbox, then connect your own when you're ready.

Stuck?

/brief-setup doesn't show up when I type it

Start a new session so Droid picks up the new skills. You can also just type "set up my morning brief".

Google or Microsoft says the app is blocked

Your company controls which apps can connect to your work account. Use a personal account tonight, or use sample data and connect your work account after asking your IT team.

The kit download failed

Ask Droid to try again. If your network blocks downloads, ask Droid to clone https://github.com/factory-ashley-jepson/morning-brief instead and copy the same four folders.

Is my email safe?

The brief is read only. It never replies, archives, deletes, or marks anything as read. The only email it sends is your brief, to you. Disconnect any app at any time under Settings, then Connectors.

After tonight

Your brief keeps arriving. To change it, just tell Droid what you want:

  • "Add my Slack DMs and mentions to my morning brief."
  • "Thirty minutes before each external meeting, send me a prep note."
  • "Every Friday at 4, send me a weekly review of what I shipped and what slipped."
  • "Draft replies for the emails in my brief and save them as drafts. Don't send anything."
