Build your own Scout

In 30 minutes, you'll build a research agent that watches the companies, people, and opportunities you care about, checks every source, and tells you why each item matters to you. No sign-ins needed.

Open this page on your laptop: factory-ashley-jepson.github.io/morning-brief

Build it like a real team 25 minutes

Start a new session, then go through four steps. Type each command in the session.

StepTypeWhat happens
1. Plan/scout-setupDroid asks four quick questions, then what you want to watch and why you care. Your answers become Scout's watchlist.
2. Build/scoutDroid searches the web, opens every source to verify it, and writes your first digest.
3. Test/scout-checkDroid reopens every link, runs a 9-point checklist, and fixes what failed.
4. Ship/scout-scheduleDroid turns it into an automation that delivers your digest on your schedule: in the Factory app, by email, or as a Slack DM.
Not sure what to watch? Pick an example in step 1: Founder, Investor, Career and opportunities, Product leader, or Women in AI opportunities. Then make it yours.

Stuck?

/scout-setup doesn't show up when I type it

Start a new session so Droid picks up the new skills. You can also just type "set up Scout".

My digest is short, or a beat is "Quiet"

That means Scout checked and found nothing new it could verify. Ask Droid to widen the window ("look at the last 30 days") or make the beat broader.

Email or Slack won't connect

Your company may require admin approval for work accounts. Choose Factory app delivery tonight, or use a personal email. You can switch later by asking Droid to edit your Scout automation.

The kit download failed

Ask Droid to try again. If your network blocks downloads, ask Droid to clone https://github.com/factory-ashley-jepson/morning-brief instead and copy the same four folders.

What can Scout see?

Only the public web. It doesn't sign in to anything. If you choose email or Slack delivery, it uses that connection only to send your digest to you.

After tonight

Your digest keeps arriving. To change it, just tell Droid what you want:

  • "Add a beat for women-led AI seed rounds, because I'm raising next quarter."
  • "End every digest with one LinkedIn post idea based on the top story."
  • "Switch my Scout to Mondays and Thursdays, and keep it under 5 items."
  • "Skip press releases unless a real outlet covered the news too."

Want more? The kit also includes a morning brief that reads your email and calendar. See extras/morning-brief on GitHub.
