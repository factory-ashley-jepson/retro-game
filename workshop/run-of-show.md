# Run of show (30 minutes)

Goal: everyone leaves with a Scout digest that passed its checks and is scheduled to arrive on its own. Nobody needs to connect an account to finish.

## Before doors open

- Setup page and QR code on the screen: `docs/qr.png`, which points to https://factory-ashley-jepson.github.io/morning-brief/
- Tonight's promo code on the screen and in `docs/index.html` (`CONFIG.promoCode`).
- One helper for every 10 to 12 attendees, each with `helper-card.md`.
- Your own Scout digest open for the demo. The Women in AI example watchlist makes a good one for this room.
- Test the Wi-Fi with a large download. The app installer is the biggest file anyone will pull.

## 0:00 to 0:03 · Why this, and the demo

Talk track: "We all try to keep up with our market, our competitors, the people we admire, and the opportunities we shouldn't miss. Most of us do it by doom-scrolling. Tonight you'll delegate that to an agent that checks its sources and tells you why each thing matters to you."

Show a finished digest. Point at three things: the "Why it matters" line on each item, the Quiet section (it tells you what it checked and found nothing), and the source link on every item.

## 0:03 to 0:07 · Setup

Put the setup page on the screen. Walk through the three setup steps: download the app, add credits, then paste the install prompt.

Say this out loud: "There's nothing to connect. Scout reads only the public web." Helpers walk the room.

## 0:07 to 0:12 · Plan: `/scout-setup`

Talk track: "Real teams start with a plan. Droid will ask what to watch and why you care. That 'why' is the most important part: it's how Scout decides what matters to you."

Suggest the example watchlists for anyone who freezes. Push people to write a real reason for each thing they watch.

## 0:12 to 0:19 · Build: `/scout`

Talk track: "Now Droid does the work. It searches, opens every page, and throws out anything it can't verify. Watch how many it rejects."

While it runs, explain: a skill is a reusable set of instructions you can share, like a recipe.

## 0:19 to 0:24 · Test: `/scout-check`

Talk track: "Never ship an agent you haven't tested. Droid reopens every link and runs a 9-point checklist: is it fresh, is it true, does the link work."

Ask: "Who got a fail? Good. Now let Droid fix it." This is the review-and-iterate moment.

## 0:24 to 0:29 · Ship: `/scout-schedule`

Talk track: "Last step: put it on a schedule. This is where a tool becomes a teammate. You choose where it lands: the Factory app, email, or Slack."

Cloud runs deliver one digest right away. Ask the room: "Hands up when your first scheduled digest lands."

## 0:29 to 0:30 · Close

"Your Scout keeps running on the schedule you picked. To change it, just ask Droid. Ideas are at the bottom of the setup page. If you want more, the kit also has a morning brief for your email and calendar." Point them to networking.

## If things go wrong

| Problem | Move |
| ------- | ---- |
| Many people stuck on install | Pair people up: one laptop, two people. Keep going. |
| `/scout` takes a long time | Normal for the first run, since it verifies every page. Keep talking. Tell people to choose "Quick" length next time. |
| Email or Slack blocked for work accounts | Switch to Factory app delivery. They can change it later. |
| Running long | Skip the check discussion. Have people run `/scout-check` and `/scout-schedule` back to back. |
| Credits error | Re-show the promo code. Helpers check that it was redeemed in the right account. |
