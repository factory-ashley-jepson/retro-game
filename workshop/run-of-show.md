# Run of show (30 minutes)

Goal: everyone leaves with a game they invented, tested, and packed into a file they can send to a friend tonight.

## Before doors open

- Setup page and QR code on the screen: `docs/qr.png`, which points to https://factory-ashley-jepson.github.io/retro-game/
- The voucher form is live (the **Get my credits** button on the setup page). Watch the voucher sheet's Status column as people claim.
- One helper for every 10 to 12 attendees, each with `helper-card.md`.
- A finished game ready to demo, plus the empty computer open in a second tab.
- Test the Wi-Fi with a large download. The app installer is the biggest file anyone will pull.

## 0:00 to 0:03 · Why this, and the demo

Talk track: "Tonight you're going to build a video game, even if you've never written a line of code. You'll work the way software teams work: plan, build, test, ship. Droid does the typing. You make the decisions."

Show the empty computer: "This is where everyone starts. The computer is on, but there's nothing inside." Then show a finished game and play it for 15 seconds.

## 0:03 to 0:07 · Setup

Put the setup page on the screen. Walk through the three setup steps: download the app, claim credits with the form (the voucher email arrives in about a minute), then paste the install prompt. When it's done, everyone sees their own empty computer say `READY.`

Helpers walk the room.

## 0:07 to 0:11 · Plan: `/plan-game`

Talk track: "Real teams start with a plan. Droid will ask you four questions and help you name your game. Your answers become the spec, and the spec is how we'll know when it's done."

Push people to make it personal: their job, their pet, their startup. The best games tonight will be inside jokes.

## 0:11 to 0:21 · Build: `/build-game`

Talk track: "Now watch your game appear, one piece at a time. First the name on the badge. Then the hero. Then the thing you dodge."

This is the longest step. Encourage people to steer: "Make the cat purple." "Make the meteors slower." Small asks work best.

## 0:21 to 0:25 · Test: `/playtest`

Talk track: "Never ship something you haven't tested. Droid plays your game and checks every item in your plan, with proof."

Ask: "Who got a fail? Good. Now ask Droid to fix it." This is the review-and-iterate moment.

## 0:25 to 0:29 · Ship: `/ship-game`

Talk track: "Last step: ship it. Droid packs your game into one file. Send it to someone right now."

Ask the room: "Hands up when you've sent your game to someone."

## 0:29 to 0:30 · Close

"Your game lives in your my-game folder. To keep going, just ask Droid: add a boss, a second level, or put it online. Ideas are at the bottom of the setup page." Point them to networking.

## If things go wrong

| Problem | Move |
| ------- | ---- |
| Many people stuck on install | Pair people up: one laptop, two people. Keep going. |
| People stuck choosing an idea | Point to the ideas on the setup page. Star Cat is the safe default. |
| Build is running long | Ask people to skip the extras. Move straight to `/playtest` with what works. |
| Running long overall | Skip the test discussion. Have people run `/playtest` and `/ship-game` back to back. |
| Credits error or no voucher email | Check spam first. Then check the voucher sheet for their email: Ashley can copy their claim link from it. |
