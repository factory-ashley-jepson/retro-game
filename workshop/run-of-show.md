# Run of show (30 minutes)

Goal: everyone leaves with a morning brief that arrives tomorrow. Anyone who can't connect a real account still builds and tests on sample data.

## Before doors open

- Setup page and QR code on the screen: `docs/qr.png`, which points to https://factory-ashley-jepson.github.io/morning-brief/
- Tonight's promo code on the screen and in `docs/index.html` (`CONFIG.promoCode`).
- One helper for every 10 to 12 attendees, each with `helper-card.md`.
- Your own brief open for the demo. Use the sample brief, or a redacted copy of your real one. Never show a real inbox on the projector.
- Test the Wi-Fi with a large download. The app installer is the biggest file anyone will pull.

## 0:00 to 0:03 · Why this, and the demo

Talk track: "Most of us start the day digging through email and calendar to figure out what needs us. Tonight you'll delegate that to an agent. By the time you leave, it'll be scheduled, and tomorrow morning it'll be in your inbox."

Show a finished brief. Point at three things: the meeting prep notes, the "Promises you made" section, and the source link on every item.

## 0:03 to 0:08 · Setup

Put the setup page on the screen. Walk through the four setup steps: download the app, add credits, connect email and calendar, then paste the install prompt.

Say this out loud: "If your work account is blocked, that's your company's policy, not you. Use a personal account, or sample data." Helpers walk the room.

## 0:08 to 0:13 · Plan: `/brief-setup`

Talk track: "Real teams start with a plan. Droid will ask you four questions. Your answers are the spec your agent follows."

Point out that Droid suggests the people you email most. That's the moment people lean in.

## 0:13 to 0:19 · Build: `/morning-brief`

Talk track: "Now Droid does the work. It reads, it doesn't send. Watch it look for promises you made in your sent mail."

While it runs, explain: a skill is a reusable set of instructions you can share, like a recipe.

## 0:19 to 0:24 · Test: `/brief-check`

Talk track: "Never ship an agent you haven't tested. Droid checks every item against the original email and runs a 10-point checklist."

Ask: "Who got a fail? Good. Now let Droid fix it." This is the review-and-iterate moment.

## 0:24 to 0:29 · Ship: `/brief-schedule`

Talk track: "Last step: put it on a schedule. This is where an assistant becomes a teammate."

Cloud runs send one brief right away. Ask the room: "Hands up when your first brief lands in your inbox."

## 0:29 to 0:30 · Close

"Your brief arrives tomorrow at the time you picked. To change it, just ask Droid. Ideas are at the bottom of the setup page." Point them to networking.

## If things go wrong

| Problem | Move |
| ------- | ---- |
| Many people stuck on install | Pair people up: one laptop, two people. Keep going. |
| Google blocks work accounts across the room | Switch everyone to sample data for Build and Test. Schedule with a personal account, or later. |
| Running long | Skip the live check discussion. Have people run `/brief-check` and `/brief-schedule` back to back. |
| Credits error | Re-show the promo code. Helpers check that it was redeemed in the right account. |
